# Sales Performance and Profitability Optimization Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales and profitability performance using GlobalMart data. The analysis focused on revenue trends, regional efficiency, sub-category margins, and discount impact to generate strategic recommendations.

## Business Problem
The business needed to understand which products and regions were driving profitability, where inefficiencies existed, and whether revenue growth was sustainable.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Excel
- CRISP-DM Framework
- Data Cleaning and Transformation

## Key Insights
- The West region generated the highest revenue and profit
- Some sub-categories had strong sales but weaker margins
- Discount levels affected profitability across products

## Recommendations
- Prioritize high-margin sub-categories
- Review discount strategy for margin-sensitive products
- Diversify growth across regions to reduce concentration risk
